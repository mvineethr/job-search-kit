Vela Retail Group — Data Analyst
Vela Retail Group runs 60 stores across Brazil and Portugal. The analytics team is based in São Paulo and works in English and Portuguese.

You will:
- Write SQL to answer questions from store operations and merchandising
- Build Tableau dashboards for regional managers
- Use Python for cleaning data and one-off analysis
- Model our transformations in dbt and explore sales trends in Looker
- Present findings to non-technical teams in Portuguese or English

Requirements:
- 3+ years as a data analyst
- Strong SQL and a good eye for dashboards
- Degree in statistics, math, economics or a related field
- Fluent Portuguese and professional English
