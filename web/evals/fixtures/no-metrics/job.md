Copperline Builders — Operations Coordinator
Copperline is a residential construction company with about 30 employees. The operations coordinator keeps the office and the job sites supplied and on schedule.

Responsibilities:
- Own scheduling for crews, inspections and deliveries
- Manage vendors and subcontractors, including quotes and follow-up
- Process invoices and match them to purchase orders
- Keep project spreadsheets in Excel current
- Enter job costs in QuickBooks and pipeline updates in Salesforce

Requirements:
- 3+ years in office operations or administration
- Strong organization and clear communication
- Experience with QuickBooks or Salesforce is a plus
